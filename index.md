## Research Experience

### DECIDE


**DECIDE: Reproducible Decisions in Agent-Augmented Databases**
*Shenzhen Institute of Computing Sciences · Advised by Prof. Wenfei Fan · Ongoing research*

AI agents can propose ways to process and query data, but adopting these proposals raises questions about execution semantics, reproducibility, and result quality. DECIDE investigates a database-controlled decision layer that checks external proposals, commits to a candidate under a fixed decision context, and supports replay and quality certification when justified by execution evidence. A central challenge is determining when evidence from individual operators supports a guarantee for the complete query, particularly when relational processing, approximate retrieval, and model inference interact. My involvement centers on related-work analysis and examining the assumptions behind these guarantees, in preparation for contributing worked examples.
