## Research Experience

### DECIDE

**Shenzhen Institute of Computing Sciences · Summer 2026**

DECIDE explores how database systems can safely and reproducibly incorporate candidate decisions generated by large language models and other external components.

My work focused on certainty guarantees, deterministic decision-making, and the relationship between agent-generated candidates and database system execution.
